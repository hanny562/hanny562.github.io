Common questions

How do I scan a receipt?

On the main screen tap Camera to photograph a receipt, or Receipt to pick a saved photo. Check the vendor, amount and date, then save. Text is recognized on your device.

How do I see costs as time?

Open Settings and set your hourly rate. Every amount then also shows the time it cost you.

How do I export or back up my data?

Settings has Export CSV and a backup-file option. The files are saved where you choose.

How does iCloud sync work? (iOS)

iCloud sync is part of Pro. It copies your data into your own private iCloud account; the developer cannot see it.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account. Delete expenses inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
