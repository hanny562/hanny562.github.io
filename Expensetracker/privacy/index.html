Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker

Last updated: today

This app is built by one person. It does not collect, store, or transmit your data anywhere. The full source of the app's claim is in the section below — written for a human, not a lawyer.

Summary in one sentence

Your receipts, budgets, and settings live only on your iPhone or Android device. They are never sent to me, Apple, Google, or anyone else.

What data the app stores

  • Receipts you save: amount, vendor, category, date, optional note, optional photo, optional sentiment ("worth it / neutral / regretted").
  • Recurring rules: vendor, amount, category, day-of-month — used to create future entries on your device.
  • Budgets: per-category monthly caps.
  • Your wage setting: optional, used to compute "hours of your life" displays.
  • A single on-device preference flag marking that the onboarding has been seen.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account. There is no email, password, or social login.
  • It does not sync anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes your data permanently — see "Backup" below for how to export first.
  • It does not run analytics or telemetry. There is no SDK that phones home.
  • It does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID). The App Store / Play Store privacy manifests reflect this.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, photos library, health data, or any other system data without an explicit in-app prompt (camera and photo-library access are only triggered when you tap "Add Receipt").

OCR (receipt text recognition)

On-device only. Android uses ML Kit text recognition, iOS uses Apple Vision. Both run entirely on your device's CPU/GPU. Captured images and recognized text never leave the device.

App Groups (iOS only)

The optional Today widget reads from the same local SwiftData store via Apple's App Group sandbox. The data still does not leave the device; the App Group is just a permission to share data between the main app and its widget extension, not with the network.

In-app purchase (optional tip jar)

If you choose to send the developer a tip, the transaction is processed by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) using their standard payment systems. The app itself never sees your card details. The transaction receipt is stored on your device in the platform-standard "purchases" database.

Backup

Your data lives only on your device. To save a backup, use the in-app Export to CSV action (Settings → Export CSV). This produces a standard .csv file that you can store anywhere — Files app, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email attachment. There is no automatic backup; you control it.

Children's privacy

The app does not target children and does not collect any data from any user. The age rating is 4+ in both stores.

Subscriptions, lifetime unlocks, and service availability

The free version of Expense Tracker is fully functional and is expected to remain available for the foreseeable future. Optional paid products are offered through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store:

  • Tip jar — a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It does not unlock any features; it is a voluntary thank-you to the developer.
  • Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly — auto-renewable subscriptions. Cancel any time in your store account settings. Both include a 14-day free trial on first activation; you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.
  • Pro Lifetime — a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks Pro features without any recurring charge.

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by the developer. Refunds are handled by the store where you made the purchase; the developer cannot issue refunds directly. Please use the standard store refund path (App Store → Account → Purchase History; Play Store → Account → Order History).

What "Lifetime" means

The "Lifetime" purchase grants you access to Pro features for as long as the app is actively available on the store you bought it from. Concretely, the developer commits to either of the following, whichever comes first:

  1. The app remains published on the store you bought Lifetime from, in which case Lifetime remains active for the version you have installed and any future versions published to that store; or
  2. The app is discontinued, in which case the developer will provide at least 90 days' notice via the app's "What's New" section, this page, and (where technically possible) an in-app message. After the 90-day notice period, Lifetime may stop functioning in some or all of its Pro features.

The developer will not voluntarily disable Lifetime features during the notice period. After 90 days, the developer may decline to maintain Pro features but will not retroactively revoke the one-time payment you made.

If the app is discontinued

If the developer decides to stop maintaining the app, they will:

  1. Announce the discontinuation at least 90 days in advance on this page, the App Store listing, the in-app "What's New" section, and the developer's contact address (the same channel used for support).
  2. Continue to make the last published build available for download from the app's existing store page, as long as the developer maintains the developer account.
  3. Refund, at their discretion, recent subscription payments for any period after the discontinuation announcement. Refunds for the one-time Lifetime purchase are not automatic, but the developer will respond to refund requests received within 30 days of the announcement.

Because the app is local-first, your data continues to work on your device even if the app is removed from the store. Use the in-app Export to CSV (Settings → Export CSV) at any time to back up your data.

Why this clause is here

This section exists to be honest about the limits of "Lifetime" rather than promise something the developer cannot guarantee in perpetuity. It is consistent with the standard practice of indie one-person software businesses, and with Apple's App Review Guidelines, which require that subscription terms be clear and not deceptive.

Changes to this policy

If the data practices ever change, the version in the app's Settings screen and the latest revision date on this page will be updated before the change ships.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at the address listed on the App Store / Play Store listing. Response time is typically within a week.
