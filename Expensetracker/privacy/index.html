Privacy Policy

Spendclock

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Spendclock is developed by Liu Guan Han, an individual developer. Contact: hanny563@gmail.com.

In one sentence

Spendclock has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no server. Your expenses are stored on your device, and the developer cannot see them.

What the app stores on your device

Expenses (amount, vendor, category, date, note, mood, an optional receipt photo, wallet, and split-bill or reimbursement details), budgets, recurring rules, auto-categorize rules, your optional hourly rate and base currency, and app settings.

Camera, photos and receipt text recognition

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or import a receipt. On Android, if you turn on the optional "Watch receipts" Quick Settings tile, the app looks for new screenshots while the tile is on. On iOS, an optional Shortcuts automation that you create can hand the app your latest screenshot. Text is recognized on your device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit's on-device model on Android). Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to the developer.

The Android app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network on its own.

Notifications

Optional daily reminders and budget alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Widgets

The home-screen widget reads your totals from the same local data on your device. It does not send anything anywhere.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The developer does not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase entitlement through Apple or Google.

Backups and sync

  • iOS iCloud sync (Pro, optional): your records are copied into your own private iCloud container using Apple CloudKit. The developer cannot access it.
  • Android: the app does not use Google cloud backup; your expenses and receipt images are excluded from it. On Android 12 and later, device-to-device transfer (for example when setting up a new phone with a cable) can still move the data directly between your two devices.
  • Files you export (CSV or a backup file) are saved wherever you choose. If you pick a cloud provider such as Google Drive in the system file picker, that file is stored under that provider's terms, and the developer cannot access it.

What the app does not do

No account, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the local data. If you enabled iCloud sync on iOS, you can delete the copy in your iCloud storage settings. The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Children

The app is not directed to children.

Changes

If these practices change, this page and its date will be updated.
